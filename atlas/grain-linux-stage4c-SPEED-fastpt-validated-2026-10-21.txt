WHAT WAS INTEGRATED
-------------------
1. FAST transforms (JPEG_XFORM_FAST) on BOTH decoder and encoder:
   jpeg_sdec_set_xform(&dec,JPEG_XFORM_FAST) + jpeg_senc_set_xform(&se,JPEG_XFORM_FAST).
   AAN IDCT/FDCT with the quantiser fused into the forward DCT (integer, no FPU).
2. CHROMA PASSTHROUGH: jpeg_sdec_init_cb_ex(...,JPEG_COEF_CHROMA,...). Cb/Cr are handed to the shim as
   QUANTISED coefficient blocks (band.coef=1) and re-encoded UNCHANGED — no IDCT/FDCT, no requant, no
   grain on chroma. Only Y is decoded to pixels, grained, and re-transformed.
3. grain_stream_band() now skips coef bands:  if(!bands[i].coef) grain_band_comp(...).  With chroma
   passthrough that means grain runs on Y only (Y.coef=0), which is also where film grain belongs.

THE BUG THAT LOOKED LIKE "grain disappeared" (sigY=0.00)  — ROOT CAUSE: STALE ARTIFACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Symptom last session: FAST+PT output was a valid NEX-3N JPEG but sigY=0.00 AND chroma MAE=0.0000
(i.e. output == input, untouched). The shim log said "4c: SKIP senc-init".
Cause: the new callback ENCODER needs ~10,496 B of scratch (align32(sizeof esctx) + JPEG_WR_BUF 4096
+ 32); g_encarena was 8 KB -> jpeg_senc_init_cb returned JPEG_ERR_ARENA -> roundtrip aborted ->
pass-through -> zero change. The fix (ENC_SCRATCH 8KB->16KB) was applied and the .so rebuilt, BUT the
log/output measured afterwards were STALE (log 11:25 was OLDER than the .so 11:27 — the fixed .so was
never re-run). So "grain gone" was never a grain bug; the 16 KB fix alone resolves it.

FIXES APPLIED (code/grain-shim/grain_hook.c)
-------------------------------------
- ENC_SCRATCH 8KB -> 16KB  (callback encoder scratch; measured need ~10.5KB).
- DEC_SCRATCH 160KB -> 256KB (chroma-passthrough decode scratch; 4:2:2 needs 137,312 B).
- sdec init switched to *_ex with JPEG_COEF_CHROMA; FAST xform set on dec+enc.
