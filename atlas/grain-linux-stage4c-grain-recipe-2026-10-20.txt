Reproduced by a FAST, integer-portable on-camera composite, validated on a real NEX-3N SOOC frame.

================= WHY NOT run filmgrain-cli on camera =================
filmgrain-cli (~/filmgrain-cli/filmgrain.py) = the Newson-Delon-Galerne Boolean/Poisson-disk
stochastic grain model (physically based, Monte-Carlo, 400 samples/px). On a 1 MP crop it took ~34-42 s on a
multicore Mac WITH numba; a 16 MP frame ~= 10 min on the Mac. On the camera ARM (single core, hundreds of MHz,
no SIMD/numba) it is HOURS — infeasible. So we BAKE the look offline and composite it cheaply on-camera.

================= THE FAST ON-CAMERA MODEL (integer-portable to the C shim) =================
Per frame, in the YCbCr domain (after decode, before encode):
  1. luma noise n = white_noise(x,y,frame_seed)  [C: a cheap integer hash PRNG per pixel; fresh seed per frame
     so grain differs every shot, like film]
  2. blur n with the SEPARABLE integer kernel K = [1,4,11,16,11,4,1] (sum 48), applied horizontally then
     vertically, then renormalize to unit variance. (This reproduces the measured autocorr:
     K gives autocorr 1.00,0.84,0.51,0.23,0.07 vs the target 1.00,0.82,0.47,0.20,0.07.)
  3. Y'  = clip(Y  + LUT_sigY[Y]  * n_luma , 0,255)
  4. Cb' = clip(Cb + LUT_sigCb[Y] * n_cb , 0,255)   n_cb = INDEPENDENT noise field, blurred COARSER (kernel
  5. Cr' = clip(Cr + LUT_sigCr[Y] * n_cr , 0,255)   n_cr = INDEPENDENT field; chroma sigma ~= luma_blur*2)
  NOTE: Cb/Cr MUST use SEPARATE noise fields from each other and from luma (decorrelated), else the colour
  mottle goes magenta/green instead of the soft NDG blue/warm mottle. Chroma grain is coarser (lower freq).
  Grain is LUMA-DOMINANT (sigY > sigCb > sigCr). All ops integer; sigma as fixed-point, kernel sum 48.

VALIDATION (fast composite vs the NDG ultramax reference, on the real crop DSC09580): grain size + intensity

================= INTEGRATION (next, once the SW codec lands) =================
The grain composite drops into the shim's roundtrip BETWEEN jpeg_decode -> {this grain pass on Y/Cb/Cr planes}
-> jpeg_encode. Port notes: integer hash PRNG (e.g. a per-pixel xorshift/wyhash of (x,y,seed)); the K blur as
integer add-shift; sigma LUTs baked as a 256-entry fixed-point table each; clip to [0,255]; per-frame seed from
a counter or gettimeofday. Luma pass is the must-have; chroma pass optional (two more noise fields). The SW codec
(decode+encode) is being built by a sonnet agent; grain is cheap on top (~1-2 madds/px).
